alte·rubi is a curated directory focused on independent gothic and indie labels. It also documents small shops, design objects, indie games, and tools for makers. Browse the archive by category or search for a particular product, label, or shop, then follow links to its official source. alte·rubi is an index, not a shop: purchases happen with the makers and retailers.
