echo-focus Lite is a macOS menu-bar app for focus and companionship. It records focus sessions, history, and statistics; offers lightweight distraction prompts based on the frontmost app and browser domain during a session; and supports character conversations through an AI service you configure yourself.

Features include a focus timer and statistics, focus prompts, AI character conversations, and rewards and personalization. Distraction detection is heuristic: it does not understand what you are actually doing and may produce false positives or miss distractions. Prompt wording requires your own AI service, and provider charges are your responsibility.

The latest version is **1.1**. It supports macOS 13 Ventura or later on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple, so macOS may block the first launch; the download page includes first-launch instructions and verification information.
