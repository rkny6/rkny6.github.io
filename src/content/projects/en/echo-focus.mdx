echo-focus is a small macOS menu-bar app. It pays attention to the app you're using, the website domain you're on, and whether your machine is idle. When you've drifted away from what you meant to do, it gives you a nudge in the voice of a character you configure.

Not just a pomodoro timer that beeps on schedule — just a nudge when you actually wander. It's currently a free beta, and I'd love for you to try it.
